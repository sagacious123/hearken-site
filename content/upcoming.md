### After service
- **Sermon notes:** the key points and themes, grouped under each passage shown
- **Recaps** as PDF, Word, .doc or a web page
- A **Recap** button on members' phones, with PDF and Word downloads

### Bibles
- **Bible library:** download Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Twi, Ewe, Swahili, French, Spanish, Portuguese and German
- Book names in each language, and copyright lines on phones and in recaps
- Two translations **side by side or stacked**

### Detection
- **Search by meaning:** verses said in other words, live and in the search box
- **Voice commands:** "next verse", "go back", "go to verse 6"
- **Singing detection:** sung lines are never mistaken for quotes or commands, and stay out of sermon notes
- References split by a pause are joined: "Esther chapter 3… let's begin from verse 10"
- Misheard and accented book names are recognised: "Exodos", "Isaya", "first Pita"
- Detections stay quick on computers where the accuracy pass can't keep up

### Screens and style
- A new **media desk layout**: Preview and Live side by side, with Go live between them
- **More screens:** a stage display for the preacher and a lower third for video switchers
- Any installed font, text outlines and stronger shadows
- Press **Enter twice** in search to put a passage straight on screen
