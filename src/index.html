Features

Everything in Hearken Studio.

All of it is included in the free trial, and works on the laptop at the media desk with no internet.

DETECTION

Hears the scripture

  • References said any way: “John three sixteen”, “John 3 16”, “John 316”, no “chapter” or “verse” needed
  • Follow-ups like “verse 18” or “chapter 4”, understood from the passage before
  • Verses read aloud without a reference
  • Verses said in other words, found by meaning
  • Misheard and accented book names: “Exodos”, “Isaya”, “first Pita”
  • Each suggestion shows how sure it is; the sure ones can go live by themselves

ACCURACY

Gets the words right

  • Speech recognised on the laptop, about half a second from word to card
  • An accuracy pass re-checks each sentence with a larger model
  • Names and words to listen for: pastors, the church, local places
  • Sermon plan: paste the notes and the day’s passages are ready and recognised faster
  • Uses the graphics chip on Apple Silicon and PCs with one

ON SCREEN

Shows it well

  • Preview first, then live with one key; Black and Clear in a hurry
  • Follow-along reading: words light up and the next verse goes up by itself
  • Two translations side by side
  • Search by reference or words, and a book, chapter and verse picker
  • Style: any installed font, colours, outlines, shadows, ready-made looks, sample backgrounds and your own photos

PHONE REMOTE

Run it from anywhere

  • Open the remote on any phone on the church Wi‑Fi
  • Previous and next verse, black screen and clear
  • See what was just detected and what’s in the sermon plan, and show any of it
  • Protected by a 4-digit PIN shown on the media desk
